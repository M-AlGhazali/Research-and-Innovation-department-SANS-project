DATA & INTERPRETATION

What you are looking at

The ground features come from Map 1's GeoPackage. Runway thresholds and width references come from SANS AIP AD 2.12. All 24 SID sequences and 50 fix coordinates were transcribed from the six retained AIP tabulation pages dated 17 September 2026.

Published vs illustrated

Fix positions, leg order and displayed restrictions are published data. The runway roll point, take-off position, climb between fixes, animation speed and aircraft are illustrative. Straight connectors visit the fixes but do not reconstruct CF/TF path behaviour, fly-over turns, ATC vectors or aircraft dynamics.

Ground is flat. Buildings use actual footprints with illustrative 12 m / 20 m heights; no surveyed terrain or building elevations are claimed. Vertical emphasis changes the display, not the altitude readout. Flight levels retain their original labels; nominal hundreds of feet are used for the animation without a weather-dependent conversion.

Source issues retained

MIGDA 3X: JN274 is absent from the 34L coordinate list; its coordinate is cross-referenced from the same-amendment 34C/34R lists. EGREP 2W: the canonical A1 reference places the 13,000 ft upper restriction at JN520. The earlier map transcription has been corrected.

Scenario (what-if) mode

Scenario mode shows simulated departures on published SIDs. Every number in it is modelled, none is measured. The noise and population model is Maha's oejn-noise-population (ECAC Doc 29, WorldPop). Fuel comes from OpenAP, with Hala's fuel and weather work as a reference. Recorded-flight mode uses Khalid's A2 pipeline, and the procedures come from the A1 dataset (SANS AIP AMDT 10/26).

Modelled noise, observed flights (1 Oct 2026)

The optional noise layer is modelled, not measured: it shows Lden, Lnight and N70 calculated from the departures observed on 1 October 2026, using ECAC Doc 29 and Maha's noise model (github.com/M40aha/oejn-noise-population). Only aggregated grids and statistics are used, with no flight tracks. Aircraft groups with fewer than 3 flights are not shown.

This is an exploration prototype. Local recorded-flight playback uses Wingbits observations when available. SID scores describe geometric agreement, not assigned clearances. Fuel and capacity models are not connected in the demo and recorded modes. Modelled fuel and noise appear only in Scenario mode. The separate noise layer is modelled from the 1 October 2026 departures and is not linked to the playback.

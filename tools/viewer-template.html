━ 34R / 16L · eastern runway
4,000 × 60 m · airport context

Two opposite end names identify one physical runway. Runway width does not describe how many aircraft may use it simultaneously.

Movement & separation facts

ATC sequences runway movements using applicable separation, including wake turbulence. This study does not assume a fixed number of aircraft per hour.

OEJN AD 2.22.2.6.1 lists independent parallel departures on 34R + 34C, 34R + 34L, or 16R + 16L. 34C + 34L is not a listed pair. These are published procedures, not a live runway status.

Ground layers & sources

Runway surfaces are threshold-derived 60 m width approximations. OSM ground geometry is community mapped. Fixed source snapshot; not for navigation.

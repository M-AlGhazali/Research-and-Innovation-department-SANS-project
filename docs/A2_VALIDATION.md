## Comparison with the acquisition notes

## Limits

This verifies structural correctness, deterministic local processing, and observable playback behavior. It does not independently certify the AIP transcription, calibrate provider measurements, validate SID assignments, prove regulatory compliance, or establish fuel/noise/capacity outcomes. Aircraft types remain unresolved unless separately sourced. Provider distribution permission and broader representativeness remain prerequisites for later research use.
